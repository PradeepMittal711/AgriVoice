1. PM-KUSUM (Component B):
    What it is: A government scheme providing a 60% subsidy for setting up standalone, off-grid solar-powered water pumps for irrigation.

    Eligibility: Individual farmers, water user associations, and farmer producer organizations (FPOs). The farmer usually must own 2-5 acres of land and must not already have a grid-connected agricultural pump.

    Documents Needed: Aadhaar card, land ownership papers (Jamabandi/Khatauni), bank passbook, passport-size photo, and a declaration stating there is no grid electricity connection.

    Application Process: Apply online through the respective State Renewable Energy Nodal Agency portal, or submit a form offline at the district agriculture or electricity office.

2. Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN):
    What it is: A direct income support scheme that provides ₹6,000 per year to farmers. The money is sent directly to bank accounts in three equal installments of ₹2,000.

    Eligibility: All landholding farmer families who own cultivable land. Excludes government employees, institutional landholders, professionals (doctors, engineers), and income-tax payers.

    Documents Needed: Aadhaar card, Aadhaar-seeded bank account details, and land ownership records.

    Application Process: Register online at the official PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in) under "New Farmer Registration," or apply locally through a Common Service Centre (CSC) or village Patwari.

3. Soil Health Card Scheme:
    What it is: A scheme that tests a farmer's soil and provides a printed card showing its nutrient status. It gives exact recommendations on which fertilizers and nutrients to apply for specific crops to improve yield and save money.

    Eligibility: Open to all farmers across India.

    Documents Needed: Aadhaar card and land details (survey/khasra number).

    Application Process: State agriculture departments collect soil samples from villages. Farmers can also contact their local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or register on the Soil Health Card portal to request testing.

4. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY):
    What it is: A comprehensive crop insurance scheme that protects farmers against financial losses from non-preventable natural risks like droughts, floods, and pests. Premium paid by farmers is capped at 2% for Kharif crops and 1.5% for Rabi crops.

    Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas, including tenant farmers and sharecroppers.

    Documents Needed: Aadhaar card, land ownership documents or tenancy agreement, bank passbook, and a crop sowing certificate (proof of sowing).

    Application Process: Apply online via the National Crop Insurance Portal (pmfby.gov.in), or offline through bank branches, CSCs, or authorized insurance agents before the crop-specific deadline.

5. Kisan Credit Card (KCC):
    What it is: A scheme providing short-term loans/credit to farmers to buy seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and equipment. The standard interest rate is 7%, but drops to 4% if the farmer repays the loan on time.

    Eligibility: Individual cultivators, tenant farmers, sharecroppers, and Self Help Groups. It is also available for dairy and fishery farmers.

    Documents Needed: Aadhaar card, PAN card, land holding documents, and passport-size photos.

    Application Process: Farmers can apply by filling out a form at any cooperative bank, regional rural bank, or commercial bank branch. Forms can also be downloaded from the PM-KISAN website.
